Taiwan / TW

Bubble Milk Tea / 珍珠奶茶

This is an iconic Tawiwanese tea-based drink that consists of brewed tea, milk, sweetener, and chewy tapioca pearls.

Origin and History

Bubble milk tea was invented in Taiwan during the 1980s. While the tea houses Chun Shui Tang and Hanlin Tea Room both claimed to have independently invented Bubble milk tea, the Taiwanese court ultimately decided that it is a shared cultural phenomenon. The innovative concept of chilled milk tea with tapioca pearls quickly gained its popularity and inflicted rivalries among tea houses, which rapidly expanded the local drink into a massive global trend.

Key Components

The tea base can either black tea, green tea, or oolong tea that are freshly brewed and shaken until it turned frothy.

The milk can be plain milk, oat milk, or any other milk alternatives.

The pearls are chewy balls made of sweet potato, starch, and brown sugar combined, creating the Q (Q弹) texture.

Japan / JP

Tapioca Tea / タピオカティー

It gained wide popularity in Japan that peaked in 2019, leading to some Japanese teenagers creating a slang called "tapiru", which literally means "to go drink tapioca."

Origin and History

Bubble tea from Taiwan first entered the Japanese market in late 1990s but remained trivial, but its popularity boomed in the late 2010s driven by youth culture.

Key Components

The tea base are Japanese teas like roasted green tea or stone-grounded green tea.

The milk can be either Hokkaido milk, oat/soy milk, or cheese foam.

The pearls can be Kokutou (brown sugar) pearls, standard black pearls, or flavored tapioca.

Thailand / TH

Thai Milk Tea / ชาเย็นใส่ไข่มุก

It is a fusion of the Tawiwanese-style bubble milk tea and the authentic Thai Iced Tea (Cha Yen)

Origin and History

It was one of the earliest adobpters of boba tea in the 1990s global wave. Rather than treating the imported boba tea as novelty, Thailand integrated the Taiwanese boba culture with their own street-sided iced tea.

Key Components

The milk can be a combo sweetened condensed milk layered on with evaporated milk, or Hokkaido milk imports from Japan.

The pearls can be mochi pearls, soft brown sugar boba, or crystal pearls.

The tea base is a heavily oxidized black tea, but commercials would sometimes add additives like warm spices, and yellow and orange food colorings blended into the dry tea leaves.
